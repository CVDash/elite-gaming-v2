CLARITY

Our slot machine with 3 screens

About Clarity

  • Server supported

  • Digital and futureproof

  • Low downtime for reporting any defects and other issues.

  • Connects to the internet using a router/firewall

  • Games are downloaded automatically on the slot machine's platform

  • Since september 2025 over 50 games can be found on the platform and new ones are being developed every year

  • Inbetween section - an addition between slot machines for customers to charge their phone, tablet or other devices

  • Supports partial digital payment - ready to be fully digitalized

  • Partial digital payment can be done through the Ticket-In-Ticket-Out system. The slot machines can be paid for using coins, tokens or a ticket with value. Winnings can be redeemed using a ticket printed by the machine, that can be inserted next to the disk or in a ticket/currency exchange machine.
