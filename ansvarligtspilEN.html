Responsible Gambling

No gambling without consideration

NO GAMBLING WITHOUT CONSIDERATION

As a gambling-related provider we have the responsibility to drive initiatives that help with preventing ludomania and intervene if it takes hold over our customers. We will work together with the other gambling industries, authorities and experts on gambling addiction, so we can create and share our vision about how we can best prevent ludomania and to create a better understanding for the consequences of said ludomania.

GAMBLING IS FOR ADULTS, NOT FOR CHILDREN

Gambling on slot machines is, in accordance to the gambling laws, illegal for people under 18.

Kids and teens under 18 must not be inside a slot machine hall, much less follow an adult inside.

MARKETING AROUND GAMBLING

We follow the rules for good marketing practices together with the rules written in the gambling industry's code of conduct.

We have worked together with the other gambling industries to develop of a code of conduct for responsible gambling, that was set in motion on the 1st of July 2019, and that contains a number of initiatives made for the consumer's protection in relation to advertisements.

We are owned by Danske Spil and a significant share of our surplus goes to support the clubs and societies in the country.
