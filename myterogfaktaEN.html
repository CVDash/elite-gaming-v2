Myths and Facts

Myths exist to be debunked

MYTHS EXIST TO BE DEBUNKED!

Many myths can be believed when it comes to gambling, gambling strategies and last but not least, winnings. A bit more down you can see some of the ones that people often hear from addicts that gamble irresponsibly, dismissing the facts about gambling in general.

Myth 1

"The more you gamble on a slot machine, the better the chance there is to win"

Fact 1

All types of gambling on a slot machine, whether physical or online, has a randomly generated result which means that there's no way to predict if or when you're going to win.

Myth 2

"After a series of gains, you know that you are "in the flow" and will continue to win"

Fact 2

There is nothing that proves that a "winner flow" even exists. Gambling is based completely on randomness.

Myth 3

"If I gamble for just a bit longer, I can win back everything that I've lost"

Fact 3

There is no guarantee that you are going to win back what you have previously lost. The most likely probability is that you will be losing even more money continuing to do so.

Myths and facts around luck.

As much as humans have gambled throughout history, hopeful people have always tried to think and measure strategies and methods to influence chances, probabilities and luck. The truth is just that when you choose to gamble, there is nothing you can do to guarantee a win!

To win in gambling is to be lucky. Everything depends on random chance!

When something happens at random means that something happened without a plan, purpose or without depending on other factors. Within gambling, which is built on complete randomness, nothing can surely predict as to what results come out the next time. Whether you try many times or whether you pulled the lever/pressed the button more slowly or more rapidly, the outcome is going to be just as random.

Can some people be blessed with luck? Are some people less lucky than others?

That's something that is actually well researched! The answer can potentially and surprisingly be "Yes!" Some people are luckier and apparently have easier access to facilities and things than others. When it comes to life as a whole, the research easily shows that to be "lucky in life" depends more on the fact that thinking like that gives a person more self-esteem and confidence, therefore causing a behaviour, where one takes more chances, tries new things a bit more often, thinks of success and seeks out possibilities. But none of that can be used for gambling. Regardless of how much confidence one can have, regardless how many fast decisions one can take, så nothing can affect how the slot machine rollers spin. In gambling, everyone has the same luck and the same chance to win.
