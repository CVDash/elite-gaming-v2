Good Habits

To gamble healthily and sensibly

Whether you gamble a little or a lot on a slot machine, we have a bucket list of simple and solid advice, which you can take advantage of. This advice helps you with the most important thing to remember: To gamble healthily and sensibly.

Set limits on time and money

Before you begin, set a limit as to how much money and how much time are you willing to gamble.The Fortuna slot machine has a function where you can choose how much time and money are you willing to spend before you start gambling. When you are nearing the limit - stop. You may want to continue, you may think that you are in luck, but always respect your limit. It's not a problem that you play, however that becomes a problem when you can't stop playing

Do not gamble for more than what you feel comfortable with telling your family or friends about.

Gambling is an expense, not an investment. Gamble only for fun, and only for the money you are willing to lose. If you gamble for more than what you feel comfortable with sharing, that's a signal that you have no idea, or no knowledge, as to how you can use your money correctly.

Have a break

The longer and more intense you gamble, the more you glue yourself to the slot machine. You are in "the zone", in "the flow". You're not just gambling, you are the gamble. It can be fun in the moment, but it's also here, it can be too fun and unhealthy.

Do not gamble to get your money back

You may need to be prepared to cut your losses when you gamble. Attempting to get your money back again through gambling will only result in a bigger loss, and it can be the start of a downwards spiral. The only winners here are those who accept that they lost.

Forget everything about skills

You cannot train and exercise to be better at winning while gambling. You are not experienced either. Wins from a slot machine rely solely on statistics and randomness.

Remember: The very fact you are following these rules will mean that you will not have a problem in the future.

On the contrary, it is a sign that you gamble healthily, and treat gambling like the entertainment it should be.

More about breaks

When it comes to education, work, training, weight loss or many other of life's offerings, we are raised to know that endurance is what it takes. But it's not like that with gambling. Actually it's quite different. Gambling has a tendency to make you more glued to the slot machine's screen, get you "in the zone" or make you "one with the game". When that happens, rational thinking stops, reason evaporates, and the sense of reality takes a break.

Therefore it is important to take breaks in order to truly gamble responsibly. It really depends on the person as to when they need to "get some fresh air" or "clear your mind", but good principles to follow could be when:

  • You have gambled intensely for more than 30 mins.
  • You have won a big prize (+1000 kr.), regardless of how little time you have gambled.
  • You have lost a significant amount of money (+500 kr.), regardless of how little time you have gambled.
  • You think gambling becomes too fun or too exciting.
  • You hear yourself cry by the slot machine's screen.
