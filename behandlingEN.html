Treatment

Get professional help

If you consider that either you or a person close to you need professional help to combat gambling addiction, there is a posibility to get in contact with one of the specialized institutions for treatment of ludomania that can be found around Denmark.

It is free to get treatment, that can be both individual as well as in a group, and that is handled either by psychologists or social workers with particular knowledge about gambling addiction. If you are a relative of a gambling addict, there are many treatment institutions that have the possibility to help with scheduling a couple or family intervention

Call the helpline for advice about gambling addiction here: (+45) 70 22 28 25
